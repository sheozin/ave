Who can register

Invited guests only

The public link takes no registrations. You invite guests from your guest list (Guests tab) and each gets a personal link to say if they are coming. With approval on, the public link takes requests for an invitation instead.

Waitlist and approval

Waitlist when full

When you reach capacity, guests can join a waitlist. Offer them places from the list below and their ticket is emailed.

Move people up automatically

When a place opens up (someone removed, a ticket refunded, capacity raised), the next people on the waitlist get it in order and their ticket is emailed. Checked every 5 minutes. A group moves up only when all of it fits, and nobody jumps the queue.

Approve each registration

Registrations wait here for you. Guests get their ticket only once you approve them.
